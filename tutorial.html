HOW TO PLAY

🎨 Placing Pixels

Select a color from the top bar or use the #COLOR button. Click anywhere on the canvas to queue a pixel. Press CONFIRM to lock it in!

🖱️ Navigation

Desktop: Click and drag to pan. Scroll to zoom.
Mobile: Pinch with two fingers to zoom. Slide one finger to pan.

⏳ The Cooldown

To prevent chaos, you must wait 5 seconds between placing pixels. Collaborate with others to build big art!
